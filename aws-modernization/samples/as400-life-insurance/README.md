# LIFE400 — Term Life Policy Administration (IBM i / AS/400)

Demo estate for **Nexus Modernize**. Mimics a 1990s IBM i term-life system:

- ILE RPG, SQLRPGLE, ILE COBOL, CL, DDS (PF/LF/DSPF)
- DB2 for i physical files
- Online 5250 programs + nightly batch
- Three business modules: **New Business & Underwriting**, **Policy Servicing & Billing**, **Claims**

This is a representative source tree for discovery, knowledge-graph, and AWS Transform intake — not a runnable IBM i compile.
